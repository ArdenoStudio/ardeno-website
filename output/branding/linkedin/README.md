# Ardeno LinkedIn artwork

Original Ardeno brand assets, Cal Sans studio lettering, and the loader's charcoal #20211F with paper #F4F4F2. The monogram texture uses the original upright A; neither the symbol nor wordmark has been redrawn.

Upload-ready PNG files:

- `ardeno-linkedin-banner-company.png` — 1512 × 256, for Ardeno's company page.
- `ardeno-linkedin-pfp.png` — 400 × 400, opaque, for the company logo/profile image.
- `ardeno-linkedin-banner-personal.png` — 1584 × 396, an additional profile-format banner.
- `ardeno-linkedin-pfp-master.png` — 1080 × 1080, a larger logo master.

`ardeno-linkedin-preview.png` is a presentation of the matching pair, not an upload banner. All upload images are PNG, below 3 MB, with the focal artwork centred and clear of the canvas edges. The A fits within a circular crop as well as a square.

Sizing checked against [LinkedIn's current company-page guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309) and [personal-profile cover guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217) on 8 October 2026. LinkedIn currently recommends a 1512 × 256 Page cover and a 400 × 400 Page logo; older third-party 1128 × 191 specifications were not used.

No artwork has been uploaded or published to LinkedIn.
